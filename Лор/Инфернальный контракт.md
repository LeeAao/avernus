# Infernal Contract

An infernal contract was a binding agreement between an individual and a devil of the Nine Hells. Such contracts usually involved a devil granting a significant boon or gift while the contractee owed a specific debt to the fiend.

## Description

Contracts varied in form and appearance. They could appear theatrically in smoke, be delivered by an imp, be written in blood on vellum, be inscribed on rapidly cooled lava-stone, or even be etched into the flesh of the bound person.

Infernal contracts were written in Infernal script. Endorsement could be as simple as signing parchment, though some contracts demanded an act of depravity or required witness by infernal notaries.

## Specifications

Once the devil and the agreeing party entered the contract willingly, the devil's boon was bestowed and the beneficiary became indebted for the specified price.

If the contractee was unwilling or unable to fulfill the bargain, penalties applied as written. Common penalties included forfeiture of material wealth, unwanted fiendish physical traits, or transfer of the mortal soul to the offended party.

## Ending Contracts

Infernal contracts could be voided if both parties agreed. In that case, both devil and contractee continued as if the contract had never existed.

Devils usually demanded major compensation before nullifying a contract. This compensation could include great wealth, soul coins, or powerful artifacts.

Contracts that had not been fully ratified could sometimes be broken by other supernatural means, though the exact rules were unclear. A devil talisman made from empyrean blood and powered by a mortal soul was one known method.

## Notable Infernal Contracts

- The agreement between the arcanaloth Fetchtatter and Bel.
- The contract between Thavius Kreeg and Zariel to save Elturel.
- The debt of Fai Chen to Mahadi of the Wandering Emporium.
- The agreement between Krydle of Baldur's Gate and the erinyes Oreasha to save Shandie Freefoot.
