# Lulu

Lulu was a hollyphant who served for centuries as war mount and friend of Zariel when Zariel was still an angel.

## Description

In her normal form, Lulu looked like a miniature elephant with golden fur and rapidly beating wings.

## Personality

Lulu was gentle and caring and believed strongly in friendship. She could not tolerate evil acts in her presence without retaliating.

## Abilities

After contact with water from the River Styx, Lulu lost her spellcasting abilities, but she could still use powerful trumpet blasts against enemies.

## History

Lulu served as Zariel's loyal companion and mount for centuries.

In 1354 DR, during the Ride, she traveled with Zariel and the Hellriders from Elturel into Avernus.

When Zariel was wounded and faced defeat, she ordered Lulu and Yael to hide the Sword of Zariel so it would not fall into enemy hands.

Facing a demon horde led by Yeenoghu, Yael and Lulu enshrined the sword in the Bleeding Citadel, a fortress of pure positive energy. The effort disoriented Lulu. She flew away and wandered Avernus for months.

She later met Mahadi, who sprinkled water from the River Styx on her face. This stripped away many of her memories and abilities. Mahadi gave her to Zariel, now transformed into an archdevil.

Zariel, unwilling to harm her former friend, sent Lulu back to Faerun. Lulu wandered the Fields of the Dead for years, where she eventually met and befriended Sylvira Savikas of Candlekeep.
