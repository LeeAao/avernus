# Thavius Kreeg

Thavius Kreeg was a human priest of Torm and ruler of Elturel and Elturgard in the late 15th century DR. He held the title High Observer and later High Overseer. Once acclaimed as the savior of Elturel, he secretly betrayed the city to Zariel, causing it and its people to be dragged into the Nine Hells.

## History

### Rise

In 1444 DR, the High Rider of Elturel was revealed as a vampire with a network of spawn, charmed servants, undead allies, and collaborators. Undead overran Elturel. The Hellriders could win by day but suffered terribly by night.

Thavius Kreeg, then a priest of Torm, pleaded to any power that would save the city. Zariel answered from a pillar of fire and offered a bargain. Elturel would be saved for fifty years, after which Zariel would take the city and its people. Kreeg also swore to serve her forever.

A second sun appeared, turning night to day and destroying the vampire lord and his spawn. Elturel was liberated. Kreeg claimed credit and was hailed as the city's savior. Only he knew the true source of the Companion.

### Reign

Kreeg later became High Observer after the disappearance of Tamal Thent, his rival and likely successor, along with her whole retinue near Boareskyr Bridge. One of his first acts was to rebuild Bridgefort and rename it Fort Tamal.

Kreeg ruled for decades and guided Elturel toward becoming the nation of Elturgard.

Around 1459 DR, he ordered the Reaching Woods barricaded after shrines to primal entities were found there. He declared the death penalty for those entering or leaving the forest.

In 1476 DR, when the entire garrison of Fort Morninglord vanished, Kreeg ordered the site bricked up and sealed. Anyone breaking in was to be declared an outlaw.

Around 1489 DR, Kreeg was aging and a leadership transition was expected. He eventually required all Elturians to swear the Creed Resolute, binding them to the city and to his contract with Zariel.

### Betrayal

In 1492 DR, the deal with Zariel was due to expire. Kreeg knew the exact time and arranged to be outside Elturel when the Companion ceased to protect it. He lured Grand Duke Ulder Ravengard of Baldur's Gate to Elturel to discuss disputes between the cities, then escaped in secret.

Shortly after, the Companion turned black and tore Elturel and its people from the Material Plane, dragging the city into Avernus.

Kreeg hid among Elturian refugees and reached Baldur's Gate. Duke Thalamra Vanthampur secretly sheltered him in Vanthampur Villa. There he examined the Shield of the Hidden Lord, which contained the imprisoned devil Gargauth. Kreeg and Vanthampur planned to release Gargauth and use him to take over Baldur's Gate, so that the city might share Elturel's fate.

Reya Mantlemorn heard rumors of Kreeg's presence and helped adventurers penetrate the villa. The adventurers uncovered the plot and recovered Kreeg's infernal puzzle box.

### Descent

If killed, Kreeg's damned soul descended to the Hells and emerged from the River Styx as a lemure. Zariel could then promote him to an amnizu as reward for his service, but imprison him in her flying fortress until Elturel was destroyed.

Killing Kreeg did not break the contract binding Elturel. To save the city by voiding the contract, the original contract had to be destroyed, Zariel slain, or some other extraordinary solution found.

## Activities

With the Order of the Companion, Kreeg maintained order in Elturel and Elturgard, aiming to bring righteous judgment to the Realms. He ensured policing, agriculture, and trade were managed efficiently, but his rule also involved harsh laws, intolerance, bans on disapproved faiths, inquisitions, and imprisonment in the Dungeon of the Inquisitor.

He ruled from the High Hall in Elturel.

## Description

By the 1490s DR, Kreeg was an old man. His shadow, however, appeared as that of a horned fiend with small wings, a visible sign of his guilt and infernal fate.

## Personality

Kreeg was deceitful, power-hungry, and eager for infernal rewards. He was a coward who avoided battle and violence when possible. If challenged, he feigned grief over Elturel's fate and lied about his involvement.

Sylvira Savikas once met him and thought him a charlatan, eventually suspecting his involvement with devils.

## Abilities

Kreeg had once been a cleric of Torm, but lost his divine power after abandoning his faith. Afterward, he only pretended to worship.

## Possessions

Kreeg had a mace and a silver holy symbol of Torm. He also possessed an infernal puzzle box containing a copy of his contract with Zariel.
