# Little One

Little One was an ogre scholar who lived and studied in Candlekeep during the late 15th century DR.

He was reticent, unlike many of his kind, and found refuge in Candlekeep after being rejected by much of civilized society. The monks of Candlekeep liked him and encouraged his intellectual pursuits.

Little One regularly stayed at the Hearth, Candlekeep's main festhall.

## History

A few years before 1494 DR, the ogre later known as Little One was as savage as any other ogre. One day, he encountered a halfling who possessed a golden headband of intellect, killed the halfling, and took the magic item.

After attuning to the headband, the ogre gained a genius-level intellect and became remorseful over the murder. He resolved to learn as much as possible, hoping to fight the ignorance and violence that had shaped his earlier life.

He adopted the name Little One in honor of the slain halfling whose death had started his new life as a scholar.

## Possessions

Little One owned the headband of intellect that transformed him. He also owned a copy of The Sum of Theology by Saint Vetheera.
