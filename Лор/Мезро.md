# Mezro

## Geography

Aliases: The Lost City.

Type: City.

Size: Metropolis.

Before its apparent destruction in the Spellplague, Mezro was the largest civilized population center in Chult. It was a holy place to the Tabaxi people and had been founded by their god Ubtao.

Population:

- 15,000 in 1363 DR.
- 28,126 in 1372 DR.

## Society

Demonym: Mezroans.

Races: Human.

Languages: Chultan.

Religion: Ubtao.

## Politics

Type: Theocracy.

Ruler type: Bara.

Known ruler: King Osaw I in 1372 DR.

Established: -2637 DR.

Disestablished: 1385 DR.

## Geography and Layout

Mezro stood along the River Olung, which formed its southern and western borders. Thick jungle lay to the east and north.

The city was circular and divided into four quarters by broad avenues that began at the Temple of Ubtao in the center.

## Agricultural Quarter

The Agricultural Quarter lay in the southeast. It contained farms, fields, and groves for bananas, berries, cassavas, nuts, papayas, plantains, sorghum, yams, and medicinal plants. Honey was also produced there. The land was magically enriched to produce fertile crops, and small dinosaurs were common.

## Residential Quarter

The Residential Quarter lay in the southwest. It was a maze of simple one-story adobe-style houses where most Mezroans lived. Its byways were made of sand and rushes.

## Market Quarter

The Market Quarter lay in the northwest. It held craftsmen, hostels, inns, and smiths. The famous amphitheater built by Ubtao himself was also there.

## Scholars' Quarter

The Scholars' Quarter lay in the northeast. It contained the Library of Mezro, the College of Wizards, the Warriors' Training Ground, and other schools. Mezro educated every child in mathematics, history, and literacy, and did not rely on guilds to teach crafts.

## Notable Locations

Amphitheater of Mezro: the center of Mezroan cultural life, decorated with gem-studded dinosaur statues and used for recitals of Chultan lore, plays, and dinosaur races.

College of Wizards: an active magical school, heavily guarded against non-students.

Library of Mezro: a three-story building holding parchment books that recorded more than fifteen hundred years of Mezroan culture, along with a museum of statues, paintings, and musical instruments.

Temple of Ubtao: an ancient magical nine-story temple with a golden dome, created by Ubtao at the center of Mezro. It contained the Hall of Champions and the king's audience hall, where the bara Osaw ruled.

## Government

Mezro was ruled by the barae, the Chosen of Ubtao. Each bara was immortal and possessed unique powers granted by Ubtao. There were seven.

At the end of the 14th century DR, the barae were:

- Osaw I.
- Kwalu.
- Dhalmass Rayburton.
- Alisanda Rayburton.
- Mainu.
- Fipya.
- Ras Nsi, exiled.

Tabaxi warriors patrolled Mezro and branded lawbreakers with a blue triangle on the forehead. Murder could be punished by execution, but most crimes were answered with compensation.

## History

Ubtao founded Mezro in -2637 DR, raising its temple and amphitheater directly from the chaos of the jungle.

In the Year of Blooded Sunsets, -137 DR, during the great war between the Tabaxi and the Eshowe, the shadow creature Eshowdow nearly ruined the city.

Around 863 DR, the whole city became invisible. A magical wall hid it and caused magical confusion in anyone who came too close. The wall remained for nearly five hundred years, until 1363 DR, when Mezro's rulers lowered the defenses after a victory over the batiri goblins. Travelers were then welcomed again, and the city grew rapidly.

In the Year of Blue Fire, 1385 DR, the Spellplague struck the world and a cataclysm separated the Chultan Peninsula from the rest of the continent. To save Mezro from destruction and from the ambitions of Ras Nsi, the barae created a demiplane and moved the city and all its citizens there. They left artificial ruins behind to discourage Ras Nsi and other threats.

According to Alisanda and the naga Saja N'baza, Mezro would not return to Toril while Ras Nsi lived.

To the rest of the world, Mezro was destroyed during the Spellplague. By 1479 DR, its artificial ruins were infested with undead and monsters. In the late 1480s DR, Flaming Fist members looted much of the ruins and began using them as training grounds for their forces in Chult.
