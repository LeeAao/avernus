# Thindol

Thindol was a land of tall grass, home to humans similar to the Chultans but renowned for extraordinary fleetness of foot.

Gold dwarves lived in the hills and mountains, their caverns ringing with weapons forged to fight hordes of kuo-toa in the waterlogged tunnels underground.

The kuo-toa were allied with yuan-ti that had the traits of swimming snakes.
