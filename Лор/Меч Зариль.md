# Sword of Zariel

The Sword of Zariel was a powerful, magical, sentient longsword.

## Description

The sword was forged from celestial steel. It constantly emitted a faint humming sound and a soft glow.

## Powers

The sword could only be used by a wielder it deemed worthy. When such a wielder attuned to it, they underwent a dramatic transformation. They became angelic, gained great beauty and a good heart, and their alignment became lawful good. These changes could not be reversed by celestial intervention or magic.

The wielder grew large angelic wings, allowing flight and hovering. Their eyes glowed faint silver, granting enhanced sight. They could see in magical and natural darkness, detect illusions, polymorphed creatures, shapechangers, invisible creatures and objects, and glimpse into the Ethereal Plane.

The sword also granted knowledge of Celestial, increased charisma and wisdom, and resistance to necrotic and radiant damage.

The sword emitted holy light in a small radius. This light hurt fiends. The wielder could brighten or dim the light. The weapon dealt radiant damage to evil creatures and could blind those it struck.

The sword was sentient and communicated through emotion. It could hear and see nearby.

## History

The sword belonged to Zariel when she was still an angel of Celestia, before her fall into the Nine Hells.
