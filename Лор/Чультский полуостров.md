# Chultan Peninsula

The Chultan Peninsula was considered by many outsiders to be a backward and unsettled land of monsters, jungles, disease, and "savages." In reality, the peninsula contained several distinct cultures and relatively stable governments that had endured for centuries.

The region's grim reputation came from its isolation from the rest of the world, the magical concealment of key cities, and the fever-induced stories of old sailors.

The Chultan Peninsula encompassed the land from the Mhair Jungles westward, including the Black Jungles, the jungles of Chult, and the countries of Tashalar, Samarach, and Thindol.
