# Fort Beluarian

## Geography

Type: Trade fort.

Region: Chult, southwest Faerun.

Size:

- Hamlet in 1372 DR.
- Small town in 1479 DR.

Fort Beluarian was located on the northern tip of Chult, northeast of Port Nyanzaru and near the eastern shore of the Bay of Chult. It received supply shipments from a nearby beach known as Beluarian Landing.

## Politics

Allegiances:

- Baldur's Gate.
- Flaming Fist.

Fort Beluarian was run by the Flaming Fist on behalf of Baldur's Gate.

## History and Population

Established: 1364 DR.

Population:

- 96 in 1364 DR.
- 313 in 1372 DR.
- 1,000 in 1479 DR.

Known rulers:

- Nenon in 1364 DR.
- Liara Portyr around 1485 DR.

## Activities

In the late 15th century DR, members of the Flaming Fist at Fort Beluarian hired adventurers to aid their efforts in Chult. Missions included expeditions to hunt undead in the jungle and reconnaissance assignments to examine remote locations or investigate strange events.

The fort also held monthly jousting tournaments.

## Trade

In the late 14th century DR, Fort Beluarian offered weary explorers and adventurers a place of refuge, at least for a night or two.

By the following century, the Flaming Fist at Fort Beluarian granted charters to adventurers and explorers who wished to enter the jungles of Chult. Mercenaries could be hired from the fort for one gold piece per soldier per tenday.

## Defenses

The fort was well supplied and strongly defended against jungle monsters.

## History

After the discovery of Maztica in 1361 DR, Duke Eltan, leader of the Flaming Fist, developed an interest in exploration. In 1364 DR he financed an expedition to Chult consisting of three ships and 150 men under the officers Beluarion and Nenon.

The expedition hired six experienced elven guides to improve its chances in the Chultan jungle, but more than half its members died, including Beluarion. The survivors founded Fort Beluarian and named it for their fallen commander.

Under Nenon's leadership, the garrison fought the local batiri tribes and made itself available for hire for non-evil ventures.

During the next century, the fort was lost twice to local uprisings and retaken both times. Amn showed interest in buying the fort, but its suspected purpose was to shut it down and drive more trade toward Amn's colony at Port Nyanzaru, creating a trade monopoly.

By the Year of the Ageless One, 1479 DR, the fort was still manned and operated by the Flaming Fist, but ownership had passed to the city of Baldur's Gate itself. Its population had grown to one thousand inhabitants. In the following years, Liara Portyr became commander of the fort under the direct command of Grand Duke Ravengard.

At some point, Liara Portyr made an agreement with the pirates of Jahaka Anchorage. This brought Fort Beluarian extra wealth and gave protection to ships sailing under its flag. The Zhentarim later heard rumors of this arrangement and sent their agent Rokah to find evidence with which to blackmail her.

## Inhabitants

The Flaming Fist hired out mercenaries from the fort for the right price, provided the task was not evil. In the late 14th century DR, the outpost was home to a small assortment of rangers, wizards, clerics, and rogues, along with numerous fighters.

By the late 15th century, the garrison was served by Castellan Gruta Halsdottir, over fifty Flaming Fist soldiers, a handful of Lords' Alliance operatives, and expert jungle guides sponsored by the Emerald Enclave.
