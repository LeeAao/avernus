# The Companion

The Companion, also known as Amaunator's Gift, was a magical second sun that hovered over Elturel in Elturgard in the latter half of the 15th century DR. It became the symbol of the realm and its defenders, the Order of the Companion.

In truth, it was the Solar Insidiator, a device created by Zariel as part of a plot to trap Elturel and steal the city into the Nine Hells.

## Description

The Companion appeared as a shining golden orb hanging directly over the High Hall at the center of Elturel. It never set or moved like the natural sun. Within the city, it shone as brightly as daylight.

It could be seen from almost anywhere in Elturgard, though its brightness faded with distance. From roughly fifty miles away it cast a pale dawn light; from farther away it looked like an unmoving star low on the horizon.

In truth, the device was a dull metal sphere about fifty feet wide, nearly indestructible. Its light was so bright that the structure itself was hidden. When corrupted, it became surrounded by impenetrable magical darkness marked inside by glowing symbols.

The Solar Insidiator was built in Bel's forge and powered by an imprisoned planetar named Nascius. Its cage was locked by nine adamantine keys once kept in Bel's forge and later stolen by Zariel's spies.

## Powers

The Companion illuminated Elturel day and night but produced no heat.

Creatures harmed by daylight were harmed by its light. It prevented undead and creatures of darkness from assaulting the city; undead found the light painful or destructive.

## History

When Elturel was overrun by the undead forces of its vampire High Rider, Thavius Kreeg begged any power to save the city. Zariel answered and offered a bargain: the city would be saved for fifty years, after which Zariel would take Elturel and its people.

The Companion appeared, destroyed the vampire threat, and remained over the city. Kreeg took credit for the miracle. The Companion brought pilgrims, paladins, and refugees from undead threats. It became central to Elturel's identity.

In 1492 DR, the Companion turned black and tore Elturel from the Material Plane, transporting it to Avernus. The corrupted orb cast baleful light over the besieged city and hurled lightning into the streets.

The Companion could ultimately be destroyed or opened by adventurers in Avernus, freeing Nascius so that the planetar could lift Elturel from its infernal bindings and return it to the Material Plane.

## Beliefs

The Companion was named because people saw it as companion to the natural sun and to Elturel's people. It was treated as an eternal promise, steady and loyal in its protection.

Many believed it was divine, but no one knew which god had sent it. Some called it Amaunator's Gift. The Creed Resolute required the Order of the Companion and Hellriders not to attribute it to any single god, so religious differences would not divide them.

Some claimed that all lands touched by the Companion's light were under Elturel's Shield, angering neighboring settlements that remained independent of Elturgard.
