# Aremag

## Basic Information

Home: Bay of Chult, Chult.

Sex: Male.

Race: Dragon turtle.

Age: Adult.

Known languages: Draconic, Aquan.

Aremag was a dragon turtle who lived in the Bay of Chult from at least the mid-14th century DR to the late 15th century DR. His activities in the bay were well known by locals of Port Nyanzaru.

## Description

Because of his fight with the Narwhal, Aremag had a blind, milky left eye and was missing a piece of his shell. He had a particularly grouchy personality.

## Personality

Aremag thought highly of himself and expected deference from sailors he encountered.

When forced into combat, he often avoided direct fighting. Instead, he knocked enemies into the water or capsized their vessels so reef sharks could devour them.

## Activities

Aremag considered a region of the Sea of Swords north of Chult to be his territory. Trade routes from Baldur's Gate and Waterdeep to Chult passed through it.

Unlike other dragon turtles around Chult, who simply enjoyed destroying every vessel they encountered, Aremag demanded tribute from any ship entering or leaving the Bay of Chult. Tribute could be treasure tossed overboard into the bay or the lives of sailors, sometimes as many as ten put out in a boat.

Ships planning to pass through the southern Sea of Swords therefore commonly carried payment for the dragon turtle. Pirates were known to capture prisoners to offer him. Aremag took the accumulated loot and stored it in his lair, whose location was unknown.

## History

In 1362 DR, the Year of the Helm, the Refuge Bay Trading Company made a deal with Aremag to pay a monthly toll for passage. After several months, Aremag broke the arrangement and threatened the Narwhal under Captain Bawr for another toll. Under protest, Bawr agreed to pay treasure, ten men of the watch who had failed to spot Aremag, and Artus Cimber for being in the way.

Artus's guardian spirit Skuld defeated Bawr and threw her into the sea as the first sacrifice. Artus then threw a magical gunstone given by Skuld, damaging Aremag's shell, and ordered ballistae to fire on the dragon turtle. Eight shots struck Aremag, and one blinded his left eye. Aremag breathed scalding steam over the ship and sank back into the sea.

The injuries did not diminish Aremag. If anything, they made him more ornery. For more than a century afterward, he continued demanding payment from passing ships and did not negotiate.

## Rumors and Legends

In the late 14th century DR, some believed that the harbormaster of Port Nyanzaru had made an agreement with Aremag, paying monthly tribute for the dragon turtle to keep all other sea monsters out of the bay.

## Background

Aremag is likely named for Gamera, a gigantic turtle-like monster from Japanese kaiju films.
