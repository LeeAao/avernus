# Zariel

Zariel was a fallen angel, archdevil, and ruler of Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells. She was once a celestial warrior consumed by hatred of fiends and the Blood War, then became one of Hell's greatest commanders.

## Description

As an archdevil, Zariel bore the marks of both celestial origin and infernal transformation. She was a winged, martial figure associated with fire, conquest, wrath, and uncompromising war.

## Personality

Zariel was driven by hatred of demons and by an obsession with the Blood War. She valued martial strength, courage, wrath, and decisive action.

Her defining flaw was that her hatred of evil and fiends became indistinguishable from a willingness to use damnation, tyranny, and infernal means to pursue victory.

Despite her fall, traces of her former angelic nature remained important to stories of redemption. The Sword of Zariel preserved part of what she had once been.

## Abilities

Zariel was a mighty archdevil and general. She commanded infernal armies, wielded devastating martial and magical power, and could grant power through infernal bargains.

## Possessions

As an angel, Zariel bore the Sword of Zariel. After her fall, her lost sword became a central relic tied to the possibility of her redemption or defeat.

## Realm

Zariel ruled Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells. Her rule was shaped by the Blood War, infernal fortresses, war machines, devil armies, and constant conflict with demons.

## Activities

Zariel focused on prosecuting the Blood War and strengthening the armies of Hell. She made infernal bargains, claimed souls, and sought soldiers for her war.

Her plot against Elturel used the Companion and the Creed Resolute to bind the city and its oath-bound defenders into her service.

## Relationships

### Archdevils

Zariel's position among the archdevils was tied to infernal politics, rivalry, and the favor of Asmodeus. Bel, former ruler and powerful weaponsmith of Avernus, remained a major figure connected to her rule and to the Solar Insidiator.

### Others

Lulu had been Zariel's companion and mount before her fall. Yael helped Lulu hide the Sword of Zariel. Thavius Kreeg served Zariel through the contract that doomed Elturel.

## Worshipers

Zariel attracted cultists, soldiers, and mortals drawn to wrath, conquest, martial glory, and infernal power. Her influence reached Baldur's Gate through plots involving Elturel, the Vanthampurs, and infernal contracts.

## History

Zariel was once an angel who became obsessed with fighting fiends and the Blood War. She led mortal forces, including the Hellriders of Elturel, into Avernus in the event remembered as the Ride.

During the campaign in Avernus, Zariel was defeated and fell. Lulu and Yael hid her sword so it would not fall into enemy hands.

After becoming an archdevil, Zariel ruled Avernus and continued the Blood War from the infernal side. She later answered Thavius Kreeg's plea to save Elturel from vampires, creating the Companion as part of a fifty-year bargain that would eventually drag the city into Avernus.

## Rumors and Legends

Legends around Zariel often focus on whether she can be redeemed, whether her angelic nature survives, and whether her sword can awaken that lost part of her.
