# Ardranatha Sheiltalah

**Ardranatha Sheiltalah** was one of the Avowed of Candlekeep who specialized in dragontaming magics, as well as the history and trade of the Vilhon Reach, active in the mid–14th and late 15th centuries DR.

## Description
When she was a young woman, still a novice scholar, in the mid–14th century DR, Ardranatha had a fair skin tone and curly chestnut hair. She wore the black baggy robes of a scholar. She was a quiet but well-respected Avowed.

## History
At a relatively young age, Ardranatha Sheiltalah of Zazesspur, Tethyr, relocated to the fortress library of Candlekeep to pursue her scholarly aspirations and became an Avowed. In one of the exercises during her days as a novice Avowed, Ardranatha was trained in proper handling of books via magics, such as the *read languages* spell. Her "practice" book was a traveling spell workbook penned by Reltar Yannatranter of Mulhorand. By the turn of the 16th century DR, Ardranatha was yet to become a senior monk, but was well on her way, being well respected among her peers.
