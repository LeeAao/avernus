# Liara Portyr

## Basic Information

Titles:

- Blaze.
- Commander of the Flaming Fist Headquarters.

Home: Baldur's Gate, Sword Coast North.

Former homes: Fort Beluarian, Chult.

Sex: Female.

Gender: Feminine.

Race: Human.

Occupation: Flaming Fist commander.

Known languages: Common, Draconic, Dwarvish.

Alignment: Lawful evil.

Challenge rating: 4.

Liara Portyr was a member of the Portyr patriar family of Baldur's Gate. In the late 15th century DR she commanded Fort Beluarian and was the highest-ranking member of the Flaming Fist in Chult. She answered only to Duke Ulder Ravengard of Baldur's Gate.

## Personality

Liara was a hard-hearted and dedicated leader who respected military order. She treated hardship as something to overcome.

She admired adventurers and supported those who worked within her jurisdiction in Chult, especially when they helped overcome obstacles relevant to her command.

## History

Liara was born into the wealthy Portyr family of Baldur's Gate and was the niece of Grand Duke Dillard Portyr.

At the age of forty-seven, Liara was assigned to Fort Beluarian in Chult. During her command, she struck a deal with the pirates of Jahaka Anchorage. The arrangement brought extra wealth to Liara and her soldiers and secured safe passage for Baldurian vessels.

The Zhentarim heard rumors of the arrangement and sent the agent Rokah to find evidence that could be used to blackmail her.

Three years into Liara's command in Chult, the death curse afflicted Toril. Liara was more concerned with her mission than with discovering the curse's origins, but she suspected that the old witch doctor Nanny Pu'pu might know something about them.

When Grand Duke Ulder Ravengard became trapped in Elturgard during Elturel's descent into Avernus in 1492 DR, Liara Portyr was summoned back to Baldur's Gate to lead the Flaming Fist.

When the Cult of Bane and its hidden leader, Lord Enver Gortash, sought political power in Baldur's Gate, the cult targeted Liara and her uncle. Gortash planned to remove Dillard from power in favor of Liara through threats, then infect Liara with an illithid tadpole that would enslave her to the Absolute.

During the Absolute crisis, Liara held the title of Commander of the Flaming Fist Headquarters at the Basilisk Gate barracks.

At that time, Blaze Portyr dispatched investigators to handle crimes in Baldur's Gate, including a plague ship case, a hand-taking murderer, and a mortal injury to a waveservant. She also supervised the investigation into the disappearance of Lora Bergauz's seven-year-old daughter from the Blushing Mermaid.

Liara found a lead in the case but was bewitched by the green hag Auntie Ethel. Her mind became clouded, and she was forced to speak in circles and forget matters related to the investigation and the missing child. If Auntie Ethel was defeated, Liara recovered her clarity, though she retained headaches and a vague memory of a fey.
