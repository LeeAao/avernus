# Janussi

Janussi was the Keeper of Tomes of Candlekeep in the late 15th century DR.

## Personality

Janussi was a devout follower of Deneir, god of literature and scholars.

## Activities

As Keeper of Tomes, Janussi was responsible for the governance of Candlekeep and for appointing scholars to leadership roles within the Avowed.

## Relationships

The Keeper of Tomes was served by the eight Great Readers of the Avowed and by First Reader Skoda Valanaster.
