# Jahaka Anchorage

## Geography

Type: Anchorage.

Region: Jahaka Bay, Chult.

Jahaka Anchorage was a 15th-century port settlement located in a secluded sea cave in Jahaka Bay along the western shore of Chult. It served as the secret anchorage for a group of pirates that raided ships in the waters surrounding Chult.

## Society

Races: humans, wereboar.

## Politics

Type: Oligarchy.

## Description

Beyond the pier, the anchorage housed living quarters, a makeshift prison, storage areas with a well-protected trove, and a tavern called Bosco's Bilge.

## Activities

The pirates used Jahaka Anchorage to store their plunder and hold captive slaves.

Their captains formed a tenuous alliance with Commander Liara Portyr of Fort Beluarian, granting her a portion of their wealth and safe passage for Baldurian ships in exchange for her turning a blind eye to their operations.

## Government

The settlement was led by the captain of whichever ship was docked in the port. Only one ship dropped anchor there at any given time.

## Defenses

Any ship nearing the anchorage had to navigate through heavy mist and a perilous circle of underwater rocks.

Beyond that, the anchorage was protected by a ballista mounted on a lookout tower built atop Bosco's Bilge.

The anchorage's treasure trove was protected by ten flying swords.

## Inhabitants

The settlement was a safehouse and temporary residence for pirates, including crew members aboard the Dragonfang, the Stirge, and the Emerald Eye.

Reef sharks inhabited the waters surrounding the sea cave.
