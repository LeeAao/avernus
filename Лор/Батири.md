# Batiri

## General Information

Race: Goblin.

Regions: Chult.

Deities: By clan.

The Batiri were a collection of goblin clans and tribes in Chult, known and feared for their tracking skills.

## Appearance

Skin color: usually green.

Typical build: typical for goblins.

Most Batiri had green skin, which blended well with the jungle surroundings. In all other ways, they appeared as typical goblins.

Batiri typically wore little more than rags. High-ranking warriors might wear poorly prepared dinosaur-hide breastplates or helmets.

## Personality

Batiri were feared by the other peoples of Chult and were considered bloodthirsty. They were especially talented at tracking through the jungle and were experts in jungle lore.

## Combat

The Batiri usually hunted at night, taking advantage of their natural darkvision. They preferred crude short bows and spears but were very adept with such weapons. A skilled warrior or general might possess a stolen metal weapon taken from a neighboring human tribe or a fallen adventurer.

Some tribes formed battle stacks in combat, with up to nine warriors standing atop one another. These stacks were used to intimidate foes or reach enemies hiding in trees.

The Batiri consumed victims of their raids or battles, provided those victims were not themselves Batiri.

Batiri also used clubs similar to maca weapons. These clubs were enhanced with razor-sharp shards of black volcanic glass, making them both bludgeoning and slashing weapons.

## Society

A Batiri clan usually consisted of 25 to 200 individuals. They rarely occupied villages, instead living nomadic lives and traveling by night from one camp to another.

At some camps, they built permanent huts over shallow pits, mainly for shade. These huts did not belong to any one clan and could be used by whichever clan happened to camp there that day. If a fight arose over who could rest in a hut, the matter was settled by blood.

The largest Batiri populations were in the regions surrounding the Valley of Lost Honor.

Although the Batiri were often considered cannibals, they only ate the flesh of other races and never members of the Batiri tribe. Eating another Batiri was punishable by death.

Batiri did not kill each other lightly, but cutting off fingers and hands was common in their internal fighting, wrestling, punching, and aggravation. When a Batiri died, the body was left to rot under the jungle sun. Kaverin Ebonhand suspected this was done less out of respect and more to attract giant rats, hyenas, vultures, and small carrion-feeding dinosaurs, which then became easy targets for Batiri arrows and spears.

In name, one queen ruled all Batiri tribes. She came to the throne and could be deposed through combat in an arena against challengers. She usually traveled with whichever clan she belonged to. Exceptionally powerful queens sometimes tried to establish a capital and have a wooden palace built.

## Religion

Every clan worshiped its own patron deity. Each Batiri deity had an avatar in one of the dangerous monsters or dinosaurs of Chult. Batiri sacrificial belief centered on encounters with a deity's chosen avatar. For example, Batiri who worshiped a god represented by a triceratops might perform a sacrifice when coming upon such a beast in the jungle by chance.

Batiri believed that volcanic eruptions were the gods spewing out food they did not like.

## History

The Batiri population grew after the great civil war between the Tabaxi and Eshowe human tribes in Chult, when the near-annihilation of the Eshowe left a vacuum for the goblin tribes to fill.

By 1372 DR, the Batiri made up about 20% of Chult's peoples.

Around 1367 DR, a rare and powerful Batiri queen, Queen M'bobo, was slain. Her death triggered a goblin war among the clans to determine her successor.

## Notable Clans

Clans were sometimes named for the deities they worshiped. Other clans identified themselves by other features.

### Dimetrodon Clan

This clan was no longer nomadic and had villages near Chultan dwarves, with whom they traded.

### The Gouged Orbs

This clan ritually gouged out an eye as part of coming-of-age ceremonies.

### Kuro

Like the Dimetrodon Clan, the Kuro were no longer nomadic and traded with Chultan dwarves. They worshiped Khurgorbaeyag, whose avatar was the gorgosaurus. In 1367 DR, the tribe was ruled by a queen named Irtana, and its shaman was her younger brother, Erytulk.

### Oogra

This clan worshiped a god whose avatar was the stegosaurus.

### Biting Ant Tribe

This clan wore stylized wooden masks resembling ants, colonies of which they kept for defense. For a time, they worshiped a shield guardian known as Vorn.

## Notes

The Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 3rd edition defines Batiri as green-skinned, while The Jungles of Chult states that Batiri could come in many different colors. Every example of Batiri in The Jungles of Chult is nevertheless green.
