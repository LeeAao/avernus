# Ubtao

Type: Primordial.

## Basic Information

Titles:

- Creator of Chult.
- Founder of Mezro.
- Father of the Dinosaurs.
- The Deceiver.

Adjective: Ubtaoan.

Aspects and aliases:

- The Jungle of Chult.
- Jungle Spirits.
- Haaashastaak, presumed by some sages.

Pantheon: Chultan pantheon.

Gender: Masculine.

Ubtao was the patron deity of Chult, the Creator of Chult, and the Founder of Mezro. He was also called the Father of the Dinosaurs. He remained distant from mortals and other deities, and his nature was unclear: he was treated as a deity, but also had primordial origins. His alleged betrayal of the primordials gave him the title "The Deceiver." After the Time of Troubles, he began to show greater interest in his followers.

## Manifestations

Favored animals: dinosaurs.

Favored minerals: gold, ivory.

Favored colors: brown, gold, green.

Other manifestations: a vision of a calm Chultan male face; a thunderous roar.

When Ubtao showed an avatar to mortals, he appeared as a powerful Tabaxi human male with dark brown skin, short cropped black hair, a trimmed goatee, and distant eyes. His age was difficult to judge. The color of his skin symbolized the fertile dark earth of his jungles.

His most common form was a massive tyrannosaurus rex, more than twice the size of the largest mortal dinosaurs. Before the 14th century DR, he appeared in Chult more often in forms other than the dinosaur avatar.

Ubtao's messages to followers were cryptic and open to interpretation. Some came through an ageless, calm, emotionless dark-skinned Tabaxi human face. After the Time of Troubles, he manifested as a thunderous roar rolling across Chult, as though every dinosaur in the jungle cried out at once. He also manifested through jungle spirits and through dinosaurs.

Some sages believed Ubtao was worshiped as Haaashastaak, Master of all Lizardkind, in southeastern Faerun. Others argued that Haaashastaak was instead an aspect of Sebek.

## Personality

Ubtao was endlessly patient and largely emotionless. He projected detachment and wisdom. Unlike many gods, he showed little interest in the daily lives of his worshipers or in the affairs of other deities.

## Powers

Ubtao could cast spells from any sphere or school of magic, but favored animal, combat, divination, elemental, plant, protection, sun, thought, and time spheres, as well as abjuration, evocation, and divination.

In dinosaur form, he was the largest and strongest creature on the Chultan Peninsula. His claws and bite were devastating, his tail could shatter walls, and his gaze could cause jungle vegetation to entangle creatures. Dinosaurs within a mile of his avatar obeyed his telepathic commands.

He was unaffected by spells from the animal, charm, and plant spheres, as well as charm and conjuration schools of magic.

## Divine Realm

Ubtao's main divine realm was the Labyrinth of Life, a vast labyrinthine jungle in the Outlands of the Outer Planes.

He also created a realm for his favored children, the dinosaurs. This realm, the Forbidden Plateau, lay in the Beastlands. Ubtao could often be found there when he wanted seclusion from his human petitioners. In that domain, he took the form of a free-roaming tyrannosaurus rex.

## Relationships

Ubtao was considered the leader of the Chultan pantheon, despite being the only power within it.

Other beings worshiped by Chultans, including spirits of nature, minor gods, and the essence of the Chultan jungle, were aspects of Ubtao himself. Because of this, he had power only in and around the jungles of Chult. Chultans who worshiped Ubtao considered dinosaurs to be "Ubtao's Children", though dinosaurs were not intelligent enough to worship their creator.

Because Ubtao was distant from other deities, he had few allies. His allies included Thard Harr, Lord of the Jungle Deeps and protector of wild dwarves, and Corellon Larethian of the elven pantheon.

Ubtao's greatest foe and antithesis was Eshowdow, born from Ubtao himself. Because of Eshowdow's nature, Ubtao could not perceive the Shadow Giant and could do little to protect his domain and followers from it. His other major enemy was Sseth, the Great Snake, a yuan-ti deity and aspect of Set.

Together with Thard Harr, Ubtao opposed Sseth and the yuan-ti, who sought to enslave both the Tabaxi and the wild dwarves of Chult.

After Dendar was imprisoned beneath the Peaks of Flame, Ubtao made an ancient pact with the gods of Faerun. He received exclusive dominion over the human inhabitants of the Chultan Peninsula, and as long as he guarded Dendar's prison, the Faerunian pantheon was to avoid spreading its influence into that part of the world.

## Worshipers

The Church of Ubtao was divided into three independent sects that venerated three aspects of Ubtao: the jungle itself, spirit worship, and Ubtao as a deity. All three were based in the jungles of Chult among the clans of Tabaxi humans.

Chultan humans and their dominant ethnicity, the Tabaxi, had worshiped Ubtao since the early days of his existence. The church included Mazewalkers, jungle druids, and Tabaxi shamans. The Ubtaoan faith was tolerant of other religions; its worshipers did not try to convert others or prevent them from venerating other deities.

Warm-blooded Chultan dinosaurs were venerated as Children of Ubtao. They were seen as harbingers of fate and carriers of Ubtao's will. If a Tabaxi hunter successfully hunted and killed several dinosaurs, Ubtaoans believed this signified the hunter's special place in Ubtao's heart. If a dinosaur killed a hunter, they believed it was punishment for disturbing the jungle's natural order.

Couatls were among the other creatures that served Ubtao.

### Rituals

Ubtao's clergy prayed for spells and miracles at noon, when the deity was at his zenith over the jungle. There were many ceremonies and holy days, especially those dedicated to dead ancestors. Many rituals were required before certain acts, such as hunting special animals or performing burials.

Small movable stone altars were used for ceremonies. When not in use, they were treated like any other rock in the jungle.

Chultan followers of Ubtao used gravestones and grave markers for their dead. When such markers were unavailable, such as after a death deep in the jungle, a large flat surface like a broad leaf could serve the same purpose. The people closest to the deceased wrote the person's name on it, while others added a single word of praise. These inscriptions served as the deceased's introduction to Ubtao when the god came to claim the soul.

### Places of Worship

The Temple of Ubtao, also called the Maze of Life, stood at the heart of Mezro and was the only major Ubtaoan place of worship. It served all three Ubtaoan sects.

## History

Ubtao shaped the land of Chult in primordial times. This act of creation was also the source of his distance from the land and his worshipers. To correct that distance, he splintered his being into aspects: spirits of nature and land. The largest part of his being became the essence of Chult's jungle, but this division left him little time to act directly among his mortal worshipers.

The creation of Chult's jungles and the splintering of Ubtao's being produced an unexpected result: Eshowdow, a dark antithesis of Ubtao and a separate divine being that sought to undo his works.

During the Shadow Epoch of the Blue Age, when gods and primordials fought for Toril, Dendar the Night Serpent devoured the sun. The Elder Gods eventually prevailed after Ubtao betrayed the primordials.

Around -24000 DR, Ubtao called on an army of couatls who worshiped him. They crossed the ocean from Katashaka and settled on the Chultan Peninsula. Around -2809 DR, the Eshowe, Tabaxi, and Thinguth human tribes followed the Ubtaoan couatls and Ecatzin from that distant continent to Chult.

Ubtao made an agreement with the Faerunian pantheon: he would guard the Peaks of Flame against the day when Dendar the Night Serpent would enter Toril and end the world. Dendar was said to be trapped behind a gigantic iron door sealing the Realm of the Dead away from Toril. In exchange, the other gods gave Ubtao sole control over Chult and agreed not to spread their faiths there.

Ubtao's followers believed that he shaped them, the animals of Chult, and the great city of Mezro. He was said to have raised Mezro from the ground for the Tabaxi people in -2637 DR. Legends claimed he lived among his creations in the city, but their constant demands drove him away. From then on, he became detached and allowed his followers to shape their own lives.

During the Time of Troubles in 1358 DR, Ubtao took the form of a tyrannosaurus hunting in Chult. His avatar fought Sseth the Great Snake in the eastern jungle. After the Godswar, Ubtao returned to the planes but became somewhat more involved with his church.

As of 1372 DR, Shar had recently absorbed Eshowdow, and this was believed to threaten the old agreement between Ubtao and the rest of the gods.

Sometime before 1400 DR, Ubtao abandoned Chult, allegedly because he was angered by his followers' infighting and dependence on him. His influence ceased to be felt. Many Chultans turned to other gods, including Waukeen, Gond, Savras, Sune, and Tymora. Without Ubtao, the nine trickster gods resurfaced in Chult; like other jungle spirits, they may have been splinters of Ubtao himself.

Some faithful believed Ubtao had not abandoned Chult, but had been driven mad by a poisonous thorn of Dendar.
