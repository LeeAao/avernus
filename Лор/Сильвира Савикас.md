# Sylvira Savikas

Sylvira Savikas was an archmage, sage, and one of the eight Great Readers of Candlekeep in the late 15th century DR. Her laboratory was located in one of Candlekeep's circular towers.

## Personality

Sylvira was extremely knowledgeable in many subjects. She had a wry sense of humor and was eager to pursue scholarly opportunities when they appeared.

## Abilities

Sylvira was exceptionally knowledgeable about the planes of existence, especially the Outer Planes as understood through the Great Wheel cosmology.

She also possessed particular expertise with magical puzzle boxes.

## Relationships

Sylvira had a quasit familiar named Jezebel.

She was an acquaintance of the archmage Traxigor, who lived in a stone tower floating above the Sea of Swords.

Falaster Fisk was her friend and agent.

## History

At some point, Sylvira came into possession of the Tome of Zariel. High Overseer Thavius Kreeg of Elturgard had told others that the book could help destroy the vampire lord and undead minions plaguing Elturel.

Sylvira traveled with Falaster Fisk and Captain Jaskir Dawn to recruit the sage Shandeir and booked passage back to Elturel aboard the Hesperornis. Their trip was interrupted when the red dragon Hoondarrh wrecked the ship at Mintarn.

In 1492 DR, adventurers investigating Thavius Kreeg's infernal puzzle box came to Sylvira in Candlekeep.

That same year, Sylvira met the Heroes of Baldur's Gate in her study. While she worked on an infernal puzzle box, a wild surge caused by Delina transported the adventurers to Elturel in the Nine Hells. Sylvira was not taken with them.
