# Shining Sea

## Geography

Type: Sea.

Region: Trackless Sea, southwest Faerun.

The Shining Sea was an arm of the Trackless Sea and one of the great seas of Faerun.

The sea stretched along the coastline of southwest Faerun, with the Chultan Peninsula to the south and Calimshan's shoreline to the north. It was large, though not as large as the Sea of Swords.

The island nation of Tharsult lay in the sea's eastern reaches. In the northeast was a massive enclosed bay known as the Lake of Steam, named for its high volcanic activity.

## Inhabitants

The Shining Sea had a large amount of trade and traffic across its surface. It was much busier than many other seas in Faerun's west.

Aquatic elves lived in the Shining Sea.

A rare and beautiful species of intelligent firebirds lived across the Shining Sea in seclusion, away from prying eyes. These birds were legendary among Chultans for their perfect beauty, but were infamous for the horrible shrieks that the birds themselves called "singing."
