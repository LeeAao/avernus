# Traxigor

Traxigor was an archmage who had been polymorphed into an otter in the late 15th century DR. His stone tower floated above the Sea of Swords.

## Relationships

Traxigor was an acquaintance of Sylvira Savikas. He took care of the hollyphant Lulu for her.

## History

Traxigor accidentally polymorphed himself into an otter. He eventually decided he preferred the new body and made the change permanent with a wish spell.

In 1492 DR, Sylvira asked Traxigor to help send an adventuring party to Avernus, and he agreed.
