# Dendar

Type: Primordial.

## Basic Information

Titles:

- Eater of the World.
- Mother of the Night Parade.
- Nidhogg.
- The Night Serpent.
- The Serpent Mother.

Dendar the Night Serpent was an elder evil and a primordial.

## 5th Edition Statistics

Alignment: Neutral evil.

Challenge rating: 30.

Realm: Fugue Plane.

## 3rd Edition Statistics

Alignment: Neutral evil.

Challenge rating: 26.

Realm: Fugue Plane.

## 2nd Edition Statistics

Alignment: Neutral evil.

Home plane: The Gray Waste.

## Description

Dendar was a colossal serpent who devoured the nightmares and fears of mortals and immortals, hoping to bring about the end of the world. She was about 300 feet long, with blue-black scales, yellow eyes, and a huge mouth filled with four fangs, spittle, and the bones of past victims.

## Powers

The Night Serpent could crush victims under her massive body or swallow them whole. Her forked tongue could trip and entangle victims, after which she could inject sleep poison and absorb their nightmares.

## Relationships

In the jungles of Chult, a cult that worshiped Ubtao opposed Dendar and called her the Eater of the World. They believed she lived beneath the Peaks of Flame and would one day emerge through a gigantic iron door to devour the sun if Ubtao failed his duty at the end of time. They considered it their sacred duty to fight her.

Another group, made up of Kelemvor worshipers, believed they were called to attack Dendar on the Fugue Plane and siphon away the nightmares she had eaten, limiting her power at the cost of their own lives.

## Worshipers

Cults devoted either to worshiping Dendar or fighting her existed almost as long as she did. Those who worshiped her kidnapped victims and used poisons to force them to dream.

The most notable cult of Dendar was the Sacred Order of Akabar in Calimport. There she was called the Serpent Mother and was believed to have caused the Night Parade.

## History

Dendar was believed to have been created when the first mortal dream occurred. Since then, she devoured the nightmares of the living. Even gods were not safe from her appetite for dreams.

The Night Serpent was believed to have ended the Blue Age and begun the Shadow Epoch by eating the sun, plunging Toril into darkness and freezing cold.

During a revolt against Cyric in the City of Strife, Gwydion the Quick challenged Dendar and wounded her with a legendary blade that had belonged to Alban Onire. As part of her concession after this defeat, Dendar unleashed the night-terrors of the Bone Castle's residents, allowing revolutionaries to storm the fortress.
