# Chultan

## General Information

Race: Human.

Regions:

- Chult.
- Tashalar.
- Samarach.
- Thindol.

Language: Chultan.

Deities:

- Ubtao.
- Eshowdow.

The term Chultan broadly referred to humans from the Chultan Peninsula, especially those descended primarily from the Tabaxi tribe.

## Appearance

Skin color: ebony.

Typical build: tall.

Chultans were tall and had very dark ebony skin, darker even than the Djen of Calimshan. They were adapted to Chult's harsh climate and did not suffer heatstroke in the same way most visiting humans did.

Chultan hair was always tightly curled.

## History

Chultans originally came from a continent southeast of Maztica and southwest of Zakhara, known as the Dark Continent or Katashaka. In -2809 DR, tribes including the Eshowe, Tabaxi, and Thinguth traveled from that homeland to Faerun. They were led by couatls who taught them of Ubtao. When they reached Chult, Ubtao met them at the Peaks of Flame.

The Thinguth settled in Thindol, while the other tribes populated the rest of the Chultan Peninsula.

According to legend, the Aldani tribe overfished lobsters in the Aldani Basin, and Ubtao cursed them for greed by turning them into lobsterfolk.

Ubtao founded the Chultan holy city of Mezro in -2637 DR.

In -438 DR, a war began between the Tabaxi and Eshowe tribes and lasted for centuries. In -137 DR, the Eshowe unleashed Eshowdow, the Shadow of the Eshowe, which sacked Mezro. After ruining the city, Eshowdow turned on the Eshowe and nearly destroyed them.

In revenge, Ras Nsi, one of the barae of Mezro, began a genocide of the remaining Eshowe. By -122 DR, the Eshowe were effectively extinct. The remaining Chultan tribes were absorbed into the Tabaxi tribe and became known as Chultans.

Before 1372 DR, Intulik of the Blood, a Chultan witch doctor, rose to political power after removing opposition. He subjugated the tribes and intended to rule for decades, but was killed by the bounty hunter Tatchalik, who later left Chult for Waterdeep.

## Outlook

Chultans as a whole distrusted power and wealth. Items too large to carry were often considered not worth owning.

Clan mattered more than the individual.

Dinosaurs were respected both as objects of worship and as sources of food and hides.

## Society

Most Chultans of the Chultan Peninsula lived in small tribal villages. No village was permitted more than one arcane spellcaster. These spellcasters brewed potions for warriors and participated in tribal ceremonies.

Villages were named for their clans, which were named after important heroes or tribal spirits. A village consisted of circular huts made from saplings tied into domes, with woven sapling walls and large-leaf roofs. Most villages included a smithy, a clan meeting house, a village altar, and family shrines.

Villages were led by councils of elders.

Rural Chultans wore simple loincloths. Chultans in Mezro wore loose robes called tobes.

Iron and steel were rare in Chult, and native Chultans rarely used such metals. Armor was uncommon because of the climate. Weapons were made from copper, obsidian, stone, or wood. Warriors wore hide breastplates and carried large colorful oval hide shields called hlang. Common weapons included the yklwa, a short spear; the kerrie, a knobbed club; handaxes; daggers; short swords; scimitars; longbows; and hunting spears. Chultans did not traditionally use cavalry.

Most rural Chultans lived by hunting and foraging. Those in the savannah also farmed and herded cattle.

## Culture

Outside Mezro, white was associated with death. Chultans in mourning wore white clothes, often simple flowing light robes.

Chultan legends about rare firebirds described their flawless beauty and the curses sent by the gods against anyone who harmed them. These stories may have discouraged hunting the birds.

## Language

The Chultan language used inhalations and clicks and had an almost musical sound.

Common Chultan male names included Atuar, Atumwa, Chiwa, Dengo, Emporo, Juma, Kunde, Kwalu, Losi, Mezoar, Mzolu, Nsi, Osaw, Rindawan, Selu, Teron, Ugor, Vazul, Weshtek, and Yapa.

Common Chultan female names included Azuil, Bati, Chuil, Dela, Eki, Fipya, Isi, Joli, Katela, Lorit, Mainu, Naboli, Nyali, Omoyala, Razira, Sana, Tefnek, U'lolo, Wadizi, Yuta, Zaidi, and Zamisi.

Chultans traditionally did not use surnames. By the late 15th century DR, some had adopted old dynastic names as surnames, including Agolo, Atazi, Balaka, Bolele, Dawa, Ekulu, Hakal, Imbogoro, Jaharwon, M'wenye, Natombe, Ngore, Oboko, O'tamu, Sahandi, Talro'a, Utugelu, Yudan, and Zuberi.

## Religion

Ubtao, god of the jungle and wild creation, was the primary god of the Chultans. He brought them from their ancient home, built their most sacred city, and taught them to walk the maze of life.

Eshowdow, unleashed against the Tabaxi people, was a part of Ubtao's essence that had been absorbed by Shar. Eshowdow continued to receive worship from a small number of Chultans after the fall of the Eshowe. Shar continued planning revenge and recruiting followers in the Valley of Lost Honor.

## Health

Chultans were susceptible to a rare natural disease that affected only certain human ethnicities. This "eating disease" caused serpent-scale-like calluses to grow on the skin, often on the forearms.
