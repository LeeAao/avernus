# Infernal Puzzle Box

An infernal puzzle box was a magical item used to protect small objects.

## Description

Infernal puzzle boxes were made from connected material sourced from the Nine Hells, often infernal iron, though some were made from bone. Each side of the box was usually about six inches wide.

## Powers

Their most common use was to protect contracts signed between devils and mortals. They could also safeguard small objects.

When an item was placed inside the box, the box sealed itself automatically and created an impenetrable magical seal. This seal also made the box immune to all damage. The box's weight did not change regardless of the weight of what was placed inside.

Once sealed, the only way to open the box was to solve its puzzle. Each box's puzzle was unique and could only be solved without magical abilities or spells. A creature that failed to solve the puzzle was struck by a painful psychic attack.

## Notable Owners

Thavius Kreeg, High Overseer of Elturel, possessed an infernal puzzle box that held the infernal contract he forged with Zariel to save his city from vampires.
