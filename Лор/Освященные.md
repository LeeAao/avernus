# Avowed

The Avowed were a non-denominational order of cloistered monks who tended and protected the great library-fortress of Candlekeep.

They came from many walks of life: clerics of knowledge gods, wizards, and even warrior-monks. They were united by scholarship and the duty to preserve the collected knowledge and lore of the Realms in all forms.

## Activities

The Avowed primarily protected the wisdom and information held within the Great Library. External threats included thieves and those seeking to destroy knowledge, but much of the order's daily work was spent fighting mold, decay, and natural deterioration.

To prevent knowledge from being lost, they purchased books or obtained them by other means.

The monks periodically sponsored expeditions to recover lost lore. When hiring adventurers, the Avowed might offer maps to treasure-filled ruins, documents about the creation of magic items, or scrolls containing rare spell specifications.

## Services

The scribes of Candlekeep offered copying and bookbinding services to Seekers. A mundane book typically cost over 100 gp to copy, while spellbooks cost from 25 to 150 gp per spell and could cost thousands of gold pieces.

Once a year, the Avowed published a small book on a single subject. These compilations were stamped with Candlekeep's sigil and attributed to the order as a whole. Copies sold in Candlekeep and major cities for 50 to 100 gp, though resale prices could become much higher.

## Members

Most Avowed were familiar with the Art, though few were spellcasters. The order included a small number of clerics of knowledge deities, a handful of wizards, and rarely paladins or warrior-monks.

## Admittance

Before taking the oath, a prospective member underwent rigorous tests and questioning. These examinations were meant to ensure that the candidate was not deceitful and would not endanger the keep or its knowledge.

## Organization

The order followed a structured hierarchy:

- **Keeper of the Tomes** - ultimate executive authority in Candlekeep. Their word was law, and every declaration was written down for future Keepers.
- **First Reader** - responsible for scholarly integrity and the expansion of the library's resources.
- **Great Readers** - eight sages forming a council over Candlekeep and its library.
- **Master Readers** - elder monks and sages who oversaw scribes and trained acolytes.
- **Scribes** - maintainers of the archives and compilers of requested information.
- **Acolytes** - junior members performing labor, cleaning, and chores. They wore black robes.

Guardianship roles included:

- **Gatewarden** - oversaw security of the keep and Great Library.
- **Watchers** - four leaders, each commanding a dozen armed monks. They wore plain brown robes.
- **Keeper of the Emerald Door** - formerly Keeper of the Portal; welcomed visitors and stood watch over the Emerald Door.

Other roles:

- **Guide** - responsible for training acolytes.
- **Chanter** - led the Endless Chant of Alaundo's prophecies.
- **Voices of the North, East, and South** - three monks accompanying the Chanter.

## Notable Members

- Ardranatha Sheiltalah - Avowed of Candlekeep in the 14th and 15th centuries DR.
- Tethtoril - First Reader after the Time of Troubles.
- Ulraunt - Keeper of the Tomes in the mid-to-late 14th century DR.
