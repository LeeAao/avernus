# Falaster Fisk

Falaster Fisk was a spy in the late 15th century DR.

## Relationships

Falaster Fisk and Sylvira Savikas of Candlekeep were good friends.

## History

At some point, Falaster accompanied Sylvira to recruit the sage Shandeir to help translate the Tome of Zariel. They worked on behalf of Thavius Kreeg, who was seeking a solution to help Elturel against its undead problem.

Their ship, the Hesperornis, was destroyed by the red dragon Hoondarrh at Mintarn, but Falaster survived.

In 1492 DR, Falaster was hired again by Sylvira to investigate Thavius Kreeg's whereabouts. Sylvira feared Kreeg had been corrupted by an archdevil.

Falaster correctly deduced that Kreeg was being sheltered by the Baldurian Vanthampur family. During reconnaissance inside their Upper City estate, he was captured.
