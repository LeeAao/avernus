# Тир, закон и справедливость

## Core Idea

Tyr's faith treats justice as disciplined, explicit, and accountable. It is not only about punishment; it is about revealing truth, recording judgment, and refusing to let law become arbitrary.

## Doctrinal Notes

- Truth should be uncovered rather than assumed.
- The guilty should be punished openly and the wrong should be repaired where possible.
- A just servant of Tyr keeps records of rulings and decisions so errors can be corrected later.
- Tyr's clergy do not obey laws they judge fundamentally unjust.
- The faith allows a narrow mercy toward genuine ignorance, which matters whenever outsiders or refugees are judged by unfamiliar rules.

## Campaign Relevance

This theme sharpens [Лираэль Хьюн](<../Characters/Лираэль Хьюн.md>)'s legal and moral frame. It also provides a useful contrast with Baldur's Gate, where law exists but is applied through status, money, and coercive discretion rather than equal justice.
